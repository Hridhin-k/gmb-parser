# More thoughtful replies

Choose **More thoughtful** beside **Shorter** in a draft's rewrite actions, on Reviews or mobile approvals. It asks for a considered response grounded in the customer's experience: acknowledge their effort and emotional impact, cover the main praise, concerns and questions, and group related points. It gives changes of focus room in short paragraphs separated by blank lines, without prescribing sentence counts, paragraph counts or a fixed sequence. The depth follows the review's substance. It should not pad a short review, repeat thanks or apologies, quote the whole review back, or invent facts, actions or promises. The action is hidden for rating-only reviews.

The selected length overrides the configured voice's ordinary sentence/word cap while keeping other brand rules. The rewrite uses the full model route, preserves the draft's language/script, retains still-valid selected store contacts and uses the existing draft persistence, audit, approval and sending flow. The result remains a draft; nothing is published by choosing the action.

For first drafts, reviews with at least 600 characters of original text receive the fuller guidance when the saved voice is not explicitly brief. Google translations are not counted twice. Explicitly brief voices remain brief until the person chooses More thoughtful. Shorter remains available to reduce the result again. Review and current-draft context limits avoid dropping the last points of a detailed review. Prompt version is v3.5.

The current draft is wording to revise, not verified business context: a customer's request to investigate must not become a claim that an investigation is already underway. Policies and actions must come from confirmed business context. The selected contact remains within a natural follow-up invitation. Paragraphing and specificity are model guidance; existing safety and contact checks still run, and users review the text before sending.
