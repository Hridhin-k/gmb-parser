# More thoughtful replies

Choose **More thoughtful** beside **Shorter** in a draft's rewrite actions, on Reviews or mobile approvals. It asks for a fuller response grounded in the customer's actual review: acknowledge their effort naturally, cover the main praise, concerns and questions, and group related points. For substantive feedback it aims for 5–8 complete sentences in one or two short paragraphs. It should not pad a short review, repeat thanks, quote the whole review back, or invent facts, actions or promises. The action is hidden for rating-only reviews.

The selected length overrides the configured voice's ordinary sentence/word cap while keeping other brand rules. The rewrite uses the full model route, preserves the draft's language/script, retains still-valid selected store contacts and uses the existing draft persistence, audit, approval and sending flow. The result remains a draft; nothing is published by choosing the action.

For first drafts, reviews with at least 600 characters of original text receive the fuller length guidance when the saved voice is not explicitly brief. Google translations are not counted twice. Explicitly brief voices remain brief until the person chooses More thoughtful. Shorter remains available to reduce the result again. Review and current-draft context limits have been increased to avoid dropping the last points of a detailed review. Prompt version is v3.4.

Length and specificity are instructions to the model, not guarantees of exact sentence counts. Existing safety and contact checks still run; users review the generated text before sending.
