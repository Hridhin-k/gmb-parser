GRM — Client guide
Google Review Management


WHAT THIS APP DOES

GRM is where your team reads and replies to Google reviews for the
businesses you manage.

You sign in, connect the Google account that manages those businesses,
and the app pulls in each business and its reviews. You can draft a
reply, approve it, and publish it back to Google.


TWO DIFFERENT GOOGLE STEPS

Signing in and connecting a business profile are not the same thing.

1. Sign in
   This only opens the app. Use Continue with Google on the login page.

2. Connect Google
   This is on Settings. It is the step that lets GRM see the Business
   Profiles that account manages.

Until Connect Google is done, Clients and Reviews stay empty.


THE SCREENS

Dashboard    Overview of reviews that need attention.
Clients      The businesses pulled from Google.
Reviews      The inbox where you read and reply.
Audit Log    A record of connections, syncs, and replies.
Settings     Connect Google, and invite teammates.


GETTING STARTED

1. Sign in with Google.
   The first time you open the app, GRM creates a workspace for you.
   A workspace is your private area. Other people do not see it unless
   you invite them.

2. Open Settings and click Connect Google.
   Choose the Google account that is an Owner or Manager of the
   businesses you want to manage. If you pick a personal Gmail that
   does not manage those businesses, the sync will come back empty.

3. Open Clients and click Sync profiles.
   GRM creates one client for each Business Profile that account can
   manage.

4. Click Sync reviews.
   Reviews appear on the Dashboard and on Reviews after this step.
   Profiles show up before reviews do.


WORKING WITH REVIEWS

Open Reviews, or start from the Dashboard.

Anyone in the workspace can write a reply or generate an AI draft.
A draft stays inside GRM until an owner or admin approves it.

Members cannot approve. Approve, and Approve & publish, are shown
only to owners and admins.

After a reply is approved, a member can publish it to Google.
Published means the reply is on Google. Until then it is only in
this app.

If publishing fails, anyone in the workspace can retry. That retry
does not approve a new draft.


INVITING A TEAMMATE

Owners and admins can invite from Settings.

Type the person's Google email, choose Member or Admin, and click
Invite. GRM does not send an email. The invite is saved against that
address.

They must sign in with that same Google email. After they sign in:

- They open your workspace.
- They see the same clients, locations, reviews, and connected
  Google accounts you see.
- They can sync, draft replies, and publish replies that are already
  approved.
- They cannot approve drafts. An owner or admin does that.
- A Member cannot invite other people. An Admin can.
- They do not see any other workspace.

If they already had their own workspace, yours opens first. A workspace
menu in the sidebar lets them switch back to their own.

Cancel a pending invite from Settings before they sign in if you
change your mind.


IF SOMETHING LOOKS EMPTY

No Google account on Settings
  Click Connect Google and finish the Google consent screen.

Connect Google says no workspace found
  Sign out, sign in again, and open Settings. A workspace is created
  the first time you enter the app.

Clients are empty after connecting
  Click Sync profiles on Clients. The connected Google account must
  be an Owner or Manager of those businesses.

Reviews are empty but clients exist
  Click Sync reviews.

A teammate signed in and sees nothing of yours
  Invite their exact Google email from your Settings, then ask them
  to sign in again with that email.
